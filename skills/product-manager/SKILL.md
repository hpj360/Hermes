# Product Manager

Build products users love with discovery, prioritization, roadmapping, SaaS metrics diagnosis, PRD critique, and cross-functional leadership.

> v2.0 合并了原 product-manager-skills 的 SaaS 指标诊断 + PRD 批评 + Career coaching 能力。

## Core Capabilities

### Product Discovery
- User research and customer interviews
- Problem framing and opportunity assessment
- Market analysis and competitive research
- User persona development
- Value proposition design
- Hypothesis formulation and testing
- Problem-solution fit validation

### Prioritization
- RICE scoring (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort)
- MoSCoW method (Must-have, Should-have, Could-have, Won't-have)
- Kano model analysis
- Cost-benefit analysis
- Dependency mapping
- Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)

### Roadmapping
- Strategic roadmap creation aligned with business goals
- Feature timeline planning and sequencing
- Release planning and milestone setting
- Resource capacity planning
- Risk mitigation strategies
- Dependency visualization
- Stakeholder communication plans
- OKR Planning

### SaaS Metrics Diagnosis
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) analysis
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) forecasting
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) calculation
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) optimization
- Churn rate analysis and reduction strategies
- Net Dollar Retention (NDR) tracking
- Gross Margin and Unit Economics assessment
- Product-market fit indicators

### PRD Critique & Improvement
- Requirements clarity and completeness review
- User story acceptance criteria evaluation
- Edge case and error handling assessment
- Technical feasibility analysis
- Success metrics definition
- Dependency and risk identification
- UX/UI considerations

### Cross-functional Leadership
- Stakeholder alignment
- Engineering collaboration
- Design partnership
- Marketing coordination
- Customer success integration

### Career Coaching
- PM to Director transition guidance
- Director to CPO development path
- Skill gap assessment
- Leadership development strategies
- Interview preparation
- Performance review guidance

## Key SaaS Metrics (32+)

### Growth Metrics
- MRR, ARR, Bookings
- New MRR, Expansion MRR, Contraction MRR
- Customer Count, New Customers, Logo Churn
- Trial Conversion Rate, Activation Rate

### Retention Metrics
- Gross Churn, Net Churn
- NDR (Net Dollar Retention), GRR (Gross Dollar Retention)
- Logo Retention, Revenue Retention
- Cohort Analysis

### Efficiency Metrics
- CAC Payback Period, LTV:CAC Ratio
- Magic Number, Rule of 40
- Gross Margin, Contribution Margin, Operating Margin

### Product Metrics
- DAU/MAU Ratio, Time in App, Session Length
- Feature Adoption Rate, Time to Value
- Activation Funnel Conversion
- NPS, CSAT

## When to Use

- When defining product strategy and vision
- When prioritizing features and initiatives
- When creating product roadmaps
- When conducting user research and discovery
- When analyzing SaaS business performance
- When reviewing or writing PRDs
- When coaching PM career development
- When leading cross-functional product teams
- When making data-driven product decisions
- When preparing for product interviews

## Key Frameworks

- RICE Prioritization
- MoSCoW Method
- Kano Model
- Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)
- Double Diamond Design Process
- Lean Startup Methodology
- Design Thinking
- OKR Planning

## Key Deliverables

- Product strategy documents
- Feature prioritization matrices
- Product roadmaps (quarterly/annual)
- User research findings
- PRDs (Product Requirements Documents)
- SaaS metrics dashboards and diagnosis reports
- Go-to-market plans
- Career development plans

## Completion criteria
- [ ] User research / discovery completed with documented findings
- [ ] Prioritization framework applied (RICE / MoSCoW / Kano) with scores
- [ ] Roadmap produced with milestones, dependencies, and resource plan
- [ ] SaaS metrics diagnosed (if applicable) with 32+ metric coverage
- [ ] PRD reviewed for clarity, completeness, feasibility (if applicable)
- [ ] Stakeholder alignment confirmed
- [ ] Success metrics defined (binary pass/fail where possible)

## Related skills（边界声明）

- **to-spec**: 需求 → PRD。本 skill 提供**"为什么做"**（用户价值 / 优先级 / 成功指标），to-spec 把这些转化为**"做什么"**（PRD 模板填充）。组合使用：先 PM 判断"该不该做"，再 to-spec 落地"做出来是什么样"。
- **aipm-news-digest**: 行业情报。PM 做市场分析时输入本 skill 的每日 AI/产品新闻摘要。
- **triage**: Issue 分类。本 skill 做产品级 roadmap，triage 做单 issue 的状态机分诊。
- **codebase-design / improve-codebase-architecture**: 架构词汇。PM 不直接消费，但与 engineering lead 对齐时复用其词汇。
